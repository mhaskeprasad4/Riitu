Frequently Asked Questions

Does handwriting matter in CA examinations?

Legibility matters far more than calligraphy. Examiners review hundreds of papers; clear headings, proper underlines under key statutory keywords, and clean ledger tables make it effortless for them to award marks.

How should working notes be formatted in CA accounts papers?

Working notes must form an integral part of your main solution, clearly numbered (e.g., Working Note 1: Calculation of Goodwill on Admission). Never calculate on rough sheets without transcribing them into the answer book.
