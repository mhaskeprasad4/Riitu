The Micro-Batch Advantage: Why 15 Students per Class is the Magic Number for CA Clearance

Exam Strategy • By CA Deepak Jain (Co-Founder & Director (FCA, Ex-PwC)) • 6 min read

A deep dive into cognitive load theory, teacher-student interaction metrics, and why smaller batch ceilings deliver verified 60%+ first-attempt pass rates. Key takeaways: In a 300-student hall, each student receives an average of 18 seconds of faculty interaction per 3-hour lecture. • In a 15-student micro-cohort, each student receives over 12 minutes of direct one-on-one attention during every session. • Faculty can detect facial hesitation and conceptual doubts instantly, resolving fundamental misunderstandings before bad habits set in. • Riitu Institution enforces a strict contractual cap of 15 seats per cohort.
