CA vs CS vs CMA: Salary, Syllabus, Career Scope & Which is Right For You After 12th?

Career & Articleship • By CA Deepak Jain (Co-Founder & Director (FCA, Ex-PwC)) • 7 min read

A definitive comparison of India’s top three commerce professions: Chartered Accountant (ICAI), Company Secretary (ICSI), and Cost & Management Accountant (ICMAI). Key takeaways: CA focuses on Financial Accounting, Auditing, and Direct/Indirect Taxation. • CS focuses on Corporate Governance, Company Law, Board Compliance, and Securities Regulations. • CMA focuses on Cost Auditing, Pricing Strategies, Factory Accounting, and Operational Management. • Average starting salary for fresh CAs in Big 4 and corporate firms ranges from ₹9.5 LPA to ₹14 LPA, with top corporate rankers commanding ₹25+ LPA.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which course is easiest among CA, CS, and CMA?

None of the three are "easy" as all are statutory professional courses governed by Acts of Parliament. However, CS involves more legal theory and qualitative drafting, while CA and CMA feature heavy mathematical and financial calculation.

Can I pursue CA and CS together?

Yes, many students pursue CA and CS simultaneously because of the immense overlap in Corporate Law, Business Law, and Economic regulations.
