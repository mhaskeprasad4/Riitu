Frequently Asked Questions

How many months are needed to clear CA Foundation after 12th?

A disciplined 5 to 6 month preparation timeline (4 to 5 hours of structured daily study and regular chapter-wise mock tests) is ideal for clearing CA Foundation on the first attempt.

What is the passing criteria for CA Foundation?

Students must score a minimum of 40% in each individual paper and an aggregate of 50% across all 4 papers (200 out of 400 total marks).

Can 12th Science students crack CA Foundation?

Absolutely. Over 30% of Riitu Institution’s top performers come from Science backgrounds. With our proprietary 14-day Science-to-Commerce bridge module, science students quickly master debit-credit logic and accounting fundamentals.
