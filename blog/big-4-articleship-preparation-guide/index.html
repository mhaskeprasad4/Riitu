How to Secure Big 4 Articleship at PwC, Deloitte, EY, and KPMG: Insider CA Guide

Career & Articleship • By CA Deepak Jain (Co-Founder & Director (FCA, Ex-PwC)) • 6 min read

Former PwC leader and Global CFO CA Deepak Jain reveals what Big 4 partners look for in CA articleship candidates, how to ace technical interviews, and common resume mistakes. Key takeaways: Big 4 firms recruit primarily during the 30-day window following CA Intermediate results. • Clearing Both Groups of CA Intermediate on your first attempt is the strongest resume signal for Big 4 shortlisting. • Technical rounds focus heavily on AS/Ind AS practical applications, CARO 2020 reporting, and GST input credit provisions. • Confidence, structured communication, and business awareness weigh equally with exam scores.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it necessary to clear Both Groups of CA Inter to get into Big 4?

While single-group candidates occasionally get shortlisted for specialized tax or advisory profiles, clearing Both Groups on the first attempt gives you a 90%+ probability of getting interview calls from PwC, Deloitte, EY, and KPMG.

What stipend do Big 4 firms pay for CA articleship in Mumbai?

Current Big 4 articleship stipends in Mumbai range from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month during the first year, escalating to ₹25,000 to ₹35,000 per month in subsequent years.
